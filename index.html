Retail AI Intelligence System

ML SYSTEM
ProblemRetail data was spread across sales, inventory, and pricing with no unified way to see demand or stock risk.

Supply Chain Risk Analytics ML System

ML SYSTEM
ProblemShipment disruptions (weather, transport mode, delays) were hard to anticipate from raw logistics data.
BuiltA risk-prediction system with a Power BI dashboard and a Streamlit app for single and bulk shipment predictions.
ApproachStar-schema PostgreSQL model, then compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost for disruption classification.
ResultLogistic Regression performed best; identified weather and delivery speed as the strongest disruption drivers.
